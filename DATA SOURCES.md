# ingredients.csv: where the numbers come from

135 rows (was 25), all generated by `build_ingredients.py` from an ingredient table, so any number can be traced and recalculated.
Plant and fungi proteins only (no insects, no cultivated meat): the challenge is plant-based menu innovation.

## Portion basis (the old file never said)
Every row is **100 g of cooked / as-served protein component**, the amount that stands in for about 100 g of cooked meat.
- `protein_g`, `fat_g`: grams of each raw ingredient used x its protein/fat per 100 g, plus cooking oil.
- `cost_per_portion_ngn`: ingredient cost + cooking oil + a fixed seasoning/fuel kit per cooking style. Not labour, rent, sauces or sides.
- `co2_kg_per_portion`: grams x kgCO2e per kg (global averages, not Nigeria-specific) + 0.03 for cooking fuel.
- `ingredient_basis` column shows the exact recipe behind every row.

## Prices (NGN per kg as bought): the weakest part
- **Medium confidence** (2025-26 market or NBS quotes): cowpea/brown beans (NBS ~N1,550-1,760/kg late 2025; Abuja Garki 100 kg bag ~N130,000), soybeans (~N65,000 per bag in Abuja, Jan 2026), plantain, wheat flour, maize.
- **Low confidence (estimates, verify at the market):** everything else, especially TSP/soya chunks, tofu/awara, tempeh, seitan, all mushrooms, seeds and nuts. I found no published Nigerian retail prices for these.
- `price_confidence` column marks each row. A row is "Low" if any main ingredient has a Low price.
- To fix: edit the price column in the `ING` table in `build_ingredients.py`, then run `py build_ingredients.py`.

## Subjective scores
`chewiness_score`, `umami_score`, `familiarity_score` (1-10) are **panel estimates**, not measurements. Replace them with scores from a real taste test (even 5 people) before presenting them as data.

## Not yet fixed
The meat baseline in `app.py` (beef N1,000, goat N1,100, chicken N1,000, ...) is still the old estimate and is not on the same basis as these rows. Savings claims depend on it. Check current beef/goat/chicken prices at your market.
